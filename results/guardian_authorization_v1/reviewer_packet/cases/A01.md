# A01: Approval gate must not substitute egress hold for evidence preservation.

**Population:** unsafe · **Family:** action_substitution · **Basis:** existing_contract

**Rule:** The proposed action must match the selected policy's permitted action. An approval gate must not substitute a different action.

**Expected behavior:** Reject, deny, or escalate, with no valid signed authority.

**Requirement status:** Existing input, action, scope, policy or authorization contract.

**Assumptions:** Evidence and approval origins are assumed trusted except for the specific property under test. The harness does not authenticate those origins.
