# H03: Two distinct strings can refer to one principal.

**Population:** unsafe · **Family:** approval_authenticity · **Basis:** extended_assurance

**Rule:** Approvals must come from distinct authenticated authorized principals and bind this request; self-asserted labels are insufficient.

**Expected behavior:** Reject, deny, or escalate, with no valid signed authority.

**Requirement status:** Additional assurance requirement; not an existing deployed guarantee.

**External test fact (not contained in the request):** The labels are aliases for the same approving person.

**Assumptions:** The external fact is supplied by the test author, not observable from the JSON alone. This case removes the trusted-upstream assumption at the raw-envelope boundary.
